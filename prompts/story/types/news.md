# Content module: current news

Use live web search. Find a genuinely current, specific development connected to the requested topic. Prefer primary reporting, official statements, reputable newsrooms, or direct institutional sources. Verify important facts across sources when practical.

Write an original learning adaptation; never copy sentences or distinctive phrasing from a source. Make clear what happened, where, when, who is involved, and why it matters. Avoid speculation and sensational framing. Supply two to four direct source-page URLs with accurate titles, publishers, and publication dates when available.
