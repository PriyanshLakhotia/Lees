# Language module: A0 / pre-A1

- Use extremely common words and concrete everyday meanings.
- Use mostly present-tense subject–verb–object sentences of roughly three to eight words.
- Introduce one idea per sentence and repeat key nouns instead of relying on pronouns.
- Avoid separable verbs, idioms, passive voice, figurative language, and subordinate clauses when possible.
- A0 is treated as controlled pre-A1 Dutch; clarity is more important than stylistic variety.
